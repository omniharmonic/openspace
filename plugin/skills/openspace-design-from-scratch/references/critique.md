# The screenshot critique

Look at every screenshot like a design director, then fix one thing at a time.

1. **Five-second test.** First screen, desktop and phone: can a stranger say what this is, who it's for and what to do next?
2. **Hierarchy.** One H1. Headline clearly biggest; supporting line second; one primary button. Eyebrows short.
3. **Direction.** Do colours, type and photos match the moodboard words and the record's rules? Does it feel like *them*, not a template?
4. **Rhythm.** Grounds alternate with intent; no three identical sections in a row; one dark or brand moment that lands.
5. **Space & alignment.** Edges line up section to section; even gaps; nothing cramped or floating.
6. **Images.** Consistent shapes within a section; faces and subjects not cropped awkwardly; nothing blurry or stretched.
7. **Type.** Two families at most; body 16–20px; lines not too long (about 70 characters); no orphaned one-word lines in headlines.
8. **Phone (390px).** Headline breaks sensibly; buttons full and tappable; sections read top to bottom; nothing overflows sideways.
9. **Truth & polish.** No sample text, sample people or placeholder links; alt text describes each picture's purpose.

Then `design_check` the page: zero errors before showing it as finished.

When you show a screenshot, ask one plain question tied to the brief ("Does this feel like a calm Sunday morning?") rather than "Do you like it?".
