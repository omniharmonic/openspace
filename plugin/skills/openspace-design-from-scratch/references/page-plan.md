# Planning the pages

Agree the plan in plain words before building. A small site done well beats a big site done thinly.

## Pages

Most first sites need 3–5 pages: **Home**, one page per main offer (Menu, Classes, Work, Services), **About**, **Visit / Contact**. A blog, shop, events or newsletter come later — they're switched on from the editor's *Finish setting up* list.

## The home page story (5–7 sections)

1. **Hello** — what it is, for whom, where; one primary action. (The signature moment: often a bespoke hero.)
2. **Proof of the thing** — the bread, the work, the space: pictures first.
3. **The offer** — what they can get or book, in three to six clear items.
4. **Why us** — the story or the approach, in their words.
5. **What people say** — real quotes only (ask for them; leave the section out if there are none).
6. **Practical** — hours, location, prices or how it works.
7. **Final action** — one clear next step on a contrasting ground.

Give each section a one-line job and a colour ground (light / light accent / dark / brand) so the rhythm alternates deliberately.

## Navigation

3–6 items, the words visitors use ("Menu", not "Our Offerings"). One button in the header for the main action.

## Copy

Use their words. Headlines 3–9 words; one or two sentences under them. If something is missing (prices, hours, a quote), ask — never invent it.
