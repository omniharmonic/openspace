## Pages

Open the **Pages** panel to see your navigation and every page.

- **Add a page** from a page template — blank, about, services, contact, blog, events, shop or link-in-bio — or duplicate an existing one. Give it a name; the address (`/about`) is filled in for you and can be changed.
- **Reorder** pages by dragging (or **⌥↑/⌥↓**), and drag a page sideways to **nest it in a folder**. Folders appear as drop-down menus.
- **Hide** a page from the navigation while keeping it published, or mark it **unlisted** so only people with the link find it.
- **Delete** a page (with confirmation). Links to it in the navigation are removed for you; the home page can't be deleted.

Badges show which pages are hidden, unlisted, password-protected or members-only.

## Page settings

Each page's settings hold:

- **Name and address.** Changing the address offers to add a **redirect** from the old one, so old links keep working.
- **Show in navigation** and **Unlisted**.
- **Who can see it** — *Everyone*, *Password* or *Members*. Password and members-only pages need the [Cloudflare hosting option](/grow/members/#hosting), because GitHub Pages can't protect pages.
- **Search & sharing** — the title and description search engines show, a sharing image, and "hide from search engines". Live previews show how the page will look in Google results and as a social card.

## Navigation, header and footer

The **Header & footer** panel (**⌥6**) controls the chrome around every page:

- **Header layout** — eight layouts, from classic logo-left to centered, split around the logo, minimal menu, side drawer and transparent over the first section.
- **Behavior** — sticky header, transparent over the hero, and the phone menu style (slide, full screen or drop-down).
- **Logo** with alt text and its size on desktop and phones.
- **Call-to-action button** at the end of the navigation — one is plenty.
- **Footer** — select it on the canvas to edit it like any section; choose its color theme and your social profiles here.

## Announcement bar and pop-up

- **Announcement bar**: a short message across the top of every page, with an optional link and a close button.
- **Pop-up**: shown after a delay, after scrolling part way, or when a visitor is about to leave. Choose how often a visitor sees it (every visit, daily, weekly, monthly or a custom number of days), and what it holds — a heading, words, an image, a button or an email signup. Pop-ups never show in the editor.

## Redirects and the 404 page

Settings → **Advanced** has a **redirects** manager (old address → new address) and lets you design your own "page not found" page. On GitHub Pages, redirects are instant HTML redirects; on Cloudflare hosting they're real 301 redirects.

## Blog, events, shop and other collections

Long-form content — blog posts, events, portfolio projects, podcast episodes and products — lives in **collections**, managed from the **Content** area. Lists, item pages, RSS and calendar feeds are generated for you when you publish. Add a collection with "Add a blog, events, shop…".
