Studio opens your home page in a live **canvas**. What you see there is rendered by the same components as your published site, so it's exactly what visitors get.

## Click anything to edit it

- **Text** edits in place: click a selected text block (or press **Enter**) and type. Headings, lists, links, quotes and emphasis work as you'd expect; text styles come from your type presets, not ad-hoc sizes.
- **Images** open the media picker — choose from your library or upload. See [Media](/guides/media/).
- **Any block** shows a toolbar: Edit text, Style, Duplicate, Move up, Move down, Delete and Ask AI.
- **Sections** have their own toolbar: move, duplicate, hide or show, color theme, Stack/Grid layout and delete.

The **inspector** on the right shows the selected block's **Content** and **Design**, or the section's **Layout** and **Design**. On phones it opens as a bottom sheet.

## Sections and presets

Pages are built from **sections** — a hero, an about block, services, testimonials, a gallery, pricing, questions, a call to action, contact details and more. To add one, hover between two sections and press **+**, or use the **Add section** panel. The gallery shows over a hundred designed **section presets**, rendered live in *your* site's styles, not as generic thumbnails.

Presets come with designed copy that shows the intended length. Replace it with your own words — keeping roughly to that length keeps the design balanced.

Each section has a **color theme**: *Light*, *Light accent*, *Dark*, *Dark accent* or *Brand*. Alternating them deliberately gives a page rhythm — for example Light → Light accent → Light → Dark for a strong finale. All five themes are generated from your palette and always keep text readable.

## Blocks

Inside a section, add **blocks** from the searchable block menu (**⌘/** or **/** on the canvas, or "Add block" in the inspector). There are more than forty: text, images, buttons, galleries, video, quotes and testimonials, forms, newsletter signup, maps, accordions, pricing tables, team grids, stats, timelines, tabs, menus, events, products, booking embeds, link lists and more.

## Two layouts: Stack and Grid

**Stack** is the default. Blocks flow top to bottom in one to four columns, with column widths and stacking order per device. It's impossible to misalign, which makes it the safe choice for most sections.

**Grid** is for precise, layered designs, like Squarespace's Fluid Engine:

- 24 columns on desktop and tablet, 8 on phones.
- Drag blocks to place them and drag the handles to resize. Rows grow to fit text, so nothing gets clipped.
- Blocks can **overlap**; change the order with **⌘]** / **⌘[** (bring forward / send backward), or **⌥⌘]** / **⌥⌘[** (to front / to back). Text overlapping text is flagged.
- Hold **⇧** to lock the drag direction, **⌥** to drop a copy, and **Esc** to cancel.
- Blocks can **bleed** to the edge of the screen.

Switch a section between Stack and Grid from its toolbar.

## Desktop, tablet and phone

Preview at **desktop (1440)**, **tablet (834)** and **phone (390)** widths from the top bar. Changes you make while previewing tablet or phone apply only to that size, and are marked with a colored dot (purple for tablet, orange for phone) — click the reset button to go back to the inherited value.

For Grid sections, the **phone layout is automatic**: blocks are stacked in visual reading order (top to bottom, then left to right). Once you rearrange it, your phone layout is kept. "Reset mobile layout" goes back to automatic.

### Editing on a phone

Studio works on phones too: edit text, swap images from your camera roll, add and reorder sections, and publish. Grid placement is edited on a tablet or computer; on phones a simple **Reorder blocks** sheet changes the phone order.

## Undo, autosave and collaborators

- **Undo and redo** without limit during a session (**⌘Z** / **⇧⌘Z**).
- Every edit is **saved automatically** — on your device instantly, and to your cloud draft within seconds. The status in the top bar says *Saved*, *Saving…* or *Saved on this device* when you're offline.
- When someone else (or Claude) is editing, you see them in the top bar and their changes appear live.

## Handy shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| **⌘K** | Command menu |
| **⌘/** or **/** | Add a block |
| **Enter** / **Esc** | Edit the selected text / select the parent |
| **⌥↑** / **⌥↓** | Move the selection up or down |
| **⌘C** / **⌘V** | Copy and paste blocks or sections (across pages too) |
| **⌘+** / **⌘−** / **⌘0** | Zoom in, out, reset |
| **Space** + drag | Pan the canvas |
| **⌥4** | Site Styles |
| **?** | All shortcuts |

On Windows and Linux, use **Ctrl** for **⌘** and **Alt** for **⌥**.

## Design check

Studio's **Design check** looks over a page the way a designer would: contrast problems, more than two typefaces, headings out of order, empty sections, images much larger than they're shown, missing alt text, text overlapping text in grid sections, pages without a clear call to action, long runs of the same color theme, and copy far outside a preset's intended length. Many issues have a one-click fix. The same rules guide Claude when it edits your site.
