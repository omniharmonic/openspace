## What every template includes

- **Pages**: home, about, a main offering page (services, work, menu, programs…), a way to get in touch, plus pages for the type of site — a journal, events, a schedule, a shop.
- **Demo content** for a fictional owner, so you see the finished state. Replace it with yours.
- **Photography** under licenses that allow redistribution, recorded in `media/LICENSES.json`.
- **Three looks** shown in the gallery.
- **Personalization**: your site name and tagline are written in when the site is created.

All fonts are openly licensed (SIL OFL). Template designs are CC BY 4.0.

## Switching looks

Because content and presentation are separate, you can try another look — even another template's look — at any time. A look is just a set of Site Styles choices (palette, fonts, spacing, corners, buttons, motion, image treatment), so your pages, words and images stay exactly where they are.

- In Studio, open **Site Styles** and change those choices directly; every change can be undone.
- With [your AI connected](/connect-your-ai/), ask for a look by name — "try Harbor's Riverside look" — and Claude applies it with `apply_template_look`, as a draft you can keep or undo.

## Making your own

Templates are data, not code, so designers can make them without writing React. See [Contributing templates](/contributing/templates/).
