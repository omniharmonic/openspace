A bakery that started in a flat kitchen

Mairi baked her first loaves for neighbours during a long winter. The queue at her door grew until it needed a shop of its own.

Today a team of six bakes through the night so the windows are full by seven. We still know most of our regulars by name — and their order.

— Mairi & the Crumb team
